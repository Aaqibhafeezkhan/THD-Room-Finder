M3 switch row (D1, D2, D4): label and an optional supporting line on the left,
the track on the right. The whole row is one `role="switch"` button, at least
44px tall.

```jsx
<Switch tone="focus" checked={false} label="Show my name" sub='Shown as "AIN 7 student"' />
<Switch checked label="Show my batch's sessions like anyone else's" />
```

`tone="focus"` on violet surfaces, `tone="primary"` in settings and sheets.
