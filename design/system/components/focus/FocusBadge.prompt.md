One focus signal on a room card or building row (D3), on its own line under
the meta.

```jsx
<FocusBadge kind="own">Your session · 24:10</FocusBadge>
<FocusBadge kind="batch">AIN 7 · 2 studying</FocusBadge>
<FocusBadge kind="claimed">Focus session · 40 min</FocusBadge>
```

Filled violet = you, violet container = your batch, dark purple = someone
else's claim.
