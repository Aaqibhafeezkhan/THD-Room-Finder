The two focus actions (D1). `tone="start"` is the filled primary pill with a
timer icon, full width on phones, in every startable state. `tone="join"` is
small and violet, inside the batch activity row.

```jsx
<FocusButton tone="start" fullWidth>Start focus</FocusButton>
<FocusButton tone="join">Join</FocusButton>
```

During a lecture render neither: hide it, don't disable it. Join starts your
own Public session; End only ever ends yours.
