Your running session from any other screen (D1, D3): trailing in the brand
app bar on phones, in the list-pane header on tablet and desktop. Opens the
room. Filled violet with the time left in the block; tonal with a cup during
a break or while the next block waits (`phase`).

```jsx
<FocusPill elapsed="32:10" room="E 212" label="Focus in E 212, 33 minutes left in block 2. Open room." />
<FocusPill elapsed="07:40" phase="break" room="E 212" label="Break in E 212, 8 minutes left. Open room." />
```

Updates once a minute under reduced motion.
