The one short state on a study table, above the topic or at the end of a row.

```jsx
<TableTag tone="focus">Starts in 10 min</TableTag>
<TableTag tone="info">Full · 4 of 4</TableTag>
<TableTag tone="alert">Cancelled by the host</TableTag>
```

focus = Soon / Now / Your table, info = Changed / Full / Maybe, alert =
Cancelled / a lecture appeared. At most one per card.
