Gatherings

A study table is a table; a gathering is a hall. Verified groups pick a room THD approved them for; the room's seat count from THabella is the capacity (13 to 300), and the hall fills as people reply. Filled violet = going, ringed light = maybe, outlined = free. Seats are not numbered. Spec: docs/product/organizations.md → Setups, Hall rules, Sizes and views.

Two setups

Long tables fill one table at a time, so people end up sitting together. Rows fill from the front, centre first; the line marks the front. Above 120 seats one dot stands for two.

Phone

The hall takes the card's width; dots stay 6 px or larger and labels 14 px or larger. A hall that would need smaller dots shows a summary and Full view, which pans and zooms.

Desktop

The hall sits at 560 px beside the details. Organizers open People beside the hall instead of a new page.
