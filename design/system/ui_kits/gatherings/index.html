Gatherings

A study table is a table; a gathering is a hall. Verified groups pick a room THD approved them for; the room's seat count is the capacity (13 to 300). Each dot is a person who replied, in the order they replied: full violet = going, half violet = maybe. The outlines only show how many more fit. Nobody reserves a seat. Spec: docs/product/organizations.md → Types, The hall, Share banner.

Share banner first

Most people meet a table or a gathering as a link preview in a group chat, so the banner is designed first: the picture big on the left (at most 48 dots), the count big on the right. No names.

Four types

The type picks the picture. Tables fill one at a time; Talk and Meeting fill row by row from the front, left to right. Above 120 people one dot stands for two.
