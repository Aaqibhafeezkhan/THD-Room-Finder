Gatherings

A study table is a table; a gathering is a hall. Verified groups pick a room THD approved them for; the room's seat count from THabella is the capacity, and the hall fills as people reply. Filled violet = going, half = maybe, outlined = free. Same words, colours and reply bar as tables. Spec: docs/product/organizations.md.

Four setups

How people in the room relate to each other decides the picture. Seats fill from the front (Stage, Rows) or table by table (Tables).
