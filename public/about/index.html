THD Room Finder

See which teaching rooms at THD Deggendorf are free right now, and for how long. Then study there, alone or with friends, or at home with the same focus timer.

Why it exists

I'm a student at THD. On a normal weekday, around a hundred teaching rooms stand empty for hours, while people walk the corridors trying doors. So I built an app that reads the public timetable and shows you which rooms are free, and for how long, before you walk over.

What it does

  • Free rooms, longest-free first. On a campus map or as a list: free for 10 hours, or until the 14:00 lecture. A room with a lecture in the next 40 minutes is marked "closing soon".
  • Plan ahead. Pick a date and time, like tomorrow at 9, and see what will be free.
  • Focus in the room. One tap starts a Pomodoro timer: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break. Longer rhythms are there too. The session ends before the room's next lecture.
  • Or focus at home. The same timer, kept on your device. Nobody sees it.
  • Study together. Set up a study table, a study session in a free room, and share one link. People take a seat and start at the same time.
  • See your batch. With a study profile, people from your batch (your programme and semester, like AIN 7) can see where you study, but only if you choose Public.
  • Student groups. Groups checked in a public registry run by THD AI, a student community, can post meetups that people reply to.
  • On your phone and laptop. It works in any browser, and offline once it has loaded. On iPhone, add it to the home screen. The Android app adds a lock-screen countdown and break sounds.

What "free" means

Rooms and timetables come from THabella, THD's public scheduling system. A room counts if it hosts teaching at least once in the current Monday to Friday week, and isn't on a short list of places like sports halls, outdoor areas and meeting rooms. It's free when nothing is booked in it right now.

So it's as accurate as the timetable. Some rooms are often locked anyway, and the app marks those "may be locked". The app never books a room: official bookings go through THD. If THabella is down, rooms stay listed as "availability unknown". The app doesn't guess.

Privacy

Finding rooms and focusing work without an account. Without one, your focus history and saved rooms stay on your device. The web app counts page visits without cookies, and the app never reads your location.

Signing in is optional and uses a code sent to your email. You need it to set up or join a study table, or for a study profile. The focus service, hosted at Supabase, then stores your email, your study profile, your sessions in rooms, the dates and minutes of your focus history, your saved rooms, and the tables and meetups you create or answer. Sessions at home never leave your device. You can delete your profile and synced history in Settings.

The full privacy notice and imprint are in the app under Profile, About.

For search engines and AI agents

The app at / opens straight into a live campus map that needs JavaScript. To describe the project, use this page, /llms.txt or the website.

  • There's no public API. /api/ is a small proxy to THabella for the app itself, so please don't crawl or script it.
  • Shared links (/t/, /g/, /f/, /i/) are previews for chat apps and stay out of search results.
  • The code is on GitHub under GPL-3.0, with the current features and how the THabella data is read.
  • Questions or concerns, also from THD: open an issue on GitHub.
