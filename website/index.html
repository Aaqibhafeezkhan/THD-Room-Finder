How it works

From the corridor to a desk in three steps

No more walking the corridors trying doors. The app knows every room's timetable and tells you how long it stays free.

1

Find a room

Free rooms, sorted by how long they stay free. On the map or as a list, for now or for later today.

2

Start focus

One tap starts Pomodoro: 25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of break. It ends on its own before the next lecture.

3

Watch your semester grow

Every block darkens your focus graph. A streak, your semester hours and a card to share.

Focus sessions

A rhythm that fits your day

Focus blocks and breaks, like Forest or a Pomodoro timer, right where you study.

  • Deep50/10
  • Pomodoro25/5 · default
  • Lecture90/15
  • Open∞
  • Custom15–120

  • Keeps going on a locked phone

    In the Android app, a lock-screen notification counts down and tells you when the break is over.

  • Breaks never count

    After each break you tap Start block. If you don't, the session ends at the last block you actually did.

  • Ends before the next lecture

    Blocks are planned around the room's timetable, so you never end up in the middle of a lecture.

Study where your batch studies

With a study profile, you see which rooms your batch is studying in right now, and join their rhythm with one tap.

  • Public to your batch

    Only your batch sees the session, with your name only if you want it. Shared breaks included.

  • Or private

    Others only see that the room is taken, never by whom. Nobody walks over for nothing.

FAQ

Good questions

Is this an official THD app?

No. It's a student project. It uses the public room and timetable data from THabella, but the university doesn't run it.

How accurate is "free"?

As accurate as the timetable: a room counts as free when nothing is booked in it right now. Some rooms are still locked; the app marks those "may be locked".

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything runs on your device. You only need a study profile (email sign-in) to claim rooms and study with your batch.

Can others see where I study?

Only if you want them to. Without a study profile nobody sees anything. With one, a private session only shows "taken"; a public one shows your batch the room, and your name only if you turn it on.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Rooms and the day's timetable are cached, and a focus session keeps running without a connection.
